About SkillMatch AI

How we extract skills, compare with job descriptions, and recommend roles.

How It Works

  • Resume parsing: your uploaded PDF/DOCX is parsed and technical skills are extracted by Python logic (plus fallbacks).
  • Matching: pasted Job Descriptions are analyzed by an NLP model to find required skills; we then compute matched / missing / extra skills and an overall score.
  • Recommendations: your confirmed skills are compared against a PostgreSQL knowledge base of roles; each role gets a score and a breakdown.

Tips for Better Results

  • Trim your skills to the most relevant ones for the target roles.
  • Paste complete, realistic JDs for better matching.
  • Add missing skills you actually have; plan to learn recurring ones.

Accuracy & Limitations

Our AI models are trained with limited public data and available compute resources, so skill extraction accuracy may vary depending on your resume format and writing style. The system generally performs above-average for common technical terms but may miss rare or domain-specific skills. If something looks off, simply edit your extracted skills before continuing — this ensures better matching and recommendations.

Privacy

Your resume or any personal information is not stored anywhere within the app. It’s processed temporarily during your session only to extract skills, and is automatically discarded once you leave or log out.
